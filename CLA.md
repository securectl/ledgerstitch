# LedgerStitch Individual Contributor License Agreement (v1.0, DRAFT)

> **DRAFT. HAVE A LAWYER REVIEW THIS BEFORE USE.** This text is adapted from the Apache Software
> Foundation Individual Contributor License Agreement v2.2 (https://www.apache.org/licenses/icla.pdf),
> the same approach Grafana Labs took for its CLA when it moved to AGPLv3
> (https://grafana.com/blog/2021/04/20/qa-with-our-ceo-on-relicensing/).
>
> Changes from the Apache text:
> - "Foundation" is replaced by the LedgerStitch company.
> - The nonprofit/bylaws clause is removed.
> - An explicit dual-licensing clause (§2.1) and an AGPL-availability commitment (§9) are added.
> - Signing moves to CLA Assistant (https://github.com/cla-assistant/cla-assistant) instead of a
>   PDF emailed to a secretary.
> - The personal-data fields are reduced to what CLA Assistant collects (GitHub identity).
>
> The Apache ICLA page states no reuse terms for its text. Counsel should confirm that adapting it
> is acceptable, and check the clauses marked [COUNSEL] below. A Corporate CLA, adapted from the
> Apache CCLA, is needed separately for contributors whose employers own their work.
>

**How to accept:** comment on your pull request with the sentence that the CLA Assistant bot
gives you:

> I have read the LedgerStitch CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA.

CLA Assistant records your GitHub username, the date and the CLA version you signed. Your
signature covers all of your future Contributions until the CLA version changes. If it changes,
the bot will ask you to re-sign.
